Training Percentages

Use these percentages of your 1RM for different training goals:

% of 1RM Weight Recommended Reps Training Goal
50-60% - 3-5 reps (4-6 sets) Explosive Power
70% - 10-15 reps (4-6 sets) Muscle Endurance
70-80% - 7-12 reps (3-5 sets) Muscle Growth (Hypertrophy)
80-100% - 1-3 reps (3-4 sets) Strength & Power

Note: These are estimates. The Epley and Brzycki formulas are the most widely used and return the same result at 10 reps. Results are most accurate when using 3-10 reps, with lower reps providing better accuracy.

Safety: Always use proper form and a spotter when attempting heavy lifts near your 1RM.
