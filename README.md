# Macro Monitor

A Progressive Web App (PWA) for calculating BMR, TDEE, macros, and One Rep Max values. Optimized for mobile devices with offline support.

## Features

- **BMR & TDEE Calculator**: Calculate your Basal Metabolic Rate and Total Daily Energy Expenditure
- **Macro Calculator**: Get personalized macro targets based on your goals
- **One Rep Max Calculator**: Calculate your 1RM using three proven formulas (Epley, Brzycki, Lombardi)
- **Reverse Training Calculator**: Generate training plans from your target 1RM with weights rounded to achievable gym plate increments (2.5kg)
- **Offline Support**: Works without internet after first load
- **Installable**: Add to your home screen like a native app

## Access the App

Visit: **https://67gedsmith.github.io/macro-monitor/**

## Installation Instructions

### Android (Using Chrome)

1. **Open Chrome** on your Android device
2. Navigate to `https://67gedsmith.github.io/macro-monitor/`
3. Tap the **menu (⋮)** in the top right corner
4. Select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**
5. Tap **"Add"** to confirm
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen

**Alternative:** Look for the install banner at the bottom of the page and tap "Install"

### iPhone/iPad (Using Safari)

1. **Open Safari** on your iPhone or iPad
2. Navigate to `https://67gedsmith.github.io/macro-monitor/`
3. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Give it a name (or keep "Macro Monitor")
6. Tap **"Add"** in the top right
7. The app icon will appear on your home screen

**Important:** You must use Safari on iOS - Chrome's "Add to Home Screen" doesn't create a proper PWA on iPhone.

## Updating the App

The app will automatically check for updates when you open it. If you're not seeing the latest version:

**On Android:**
1. Long-press the app icon → App info → Storage → Clear cache
2. Reopen the app

**On iPhone:**
1. Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → Find the app → Swipe to delete
2. Reopen the app

Or simply pull down to refresh the app, then close and reopen it.
